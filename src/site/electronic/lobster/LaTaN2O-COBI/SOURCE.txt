Real LOBSTER output for LaTaN2O (5 atoms, spin-restricted, orbital-resolved crystal orbital bond indices, LOBSTER 4 list format) from the [pymatgen test files](https://github.com/materialsproject/pymatgen-test-files/tree/85005108e99e40cbd59e78f067459b0ca664efc6/electronic_structure/cohp) (COBICAR.lobster.gz, ICOBILIST.lobster, POSCAR.COBI). COBICAR keeps every 10th energy (0.1 eV steps through E_F) from −7.9 eV, where the bonds' COBI starts, and drops the 48 orbital pairs whose COBI is zero at every energy; its header is updated to match.
